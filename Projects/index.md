<div><h3>Redlining and the HOLC</h3><p>I tabulated the Home Owners&rsquo; Loan Corporation&rsquo;s Area Descriptions and use them to rethink what the &ldquo;redlining&rdquo; maps were for and how race was built into theories of real estate value.</p></div>
</a>

<img src="/assets/img/hhuud.jpg" alt="Map of housing unit change by census tract near Atlanta">
<div><h3>Historical housing and urbanization</h3><p>HHUUD10 estimates housing units and urbanization in consistent census tracts for the continental US from 1940 to 2019, opening up the historical geographies of urban renewal and sub/urbanization.</p></div>
</a>
<a class="row" href="/Projects/suburbs/">
<img src="/assets/img/suburbs.jpg" alt="Overgrown sign of a demolished apartment complex in suburban Atlanta">
<div><h3>Sub/urban displacement</h3><p>My earliest work followed the apartment demolitions that pushed thousands of Latino and Black residents out of Atlanta&rsquo;s northern suburbs in the name of New Urbanist redevelopment.</p></div>
