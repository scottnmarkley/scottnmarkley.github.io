### [National Zoning Atlas](https://doi.org/10.4079/zoning-atlas)

> Standardized zoning data for more than 11,000 US jurisdictions, with over 120 variables per zoning district, shown on the [public Atlas](https://www.zoningatlas.org/atlas) and summarized in the NZA&rsquo;s [snapshots](https://www.zoningatlas.org/snapshots) and [geographic reports](/Projects/NZA/). The Atlas aims to digitize, demystify, and democratize zoning codes. Methods: [*How to Make a Zoning Atlas 2.0*](http://dx.doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4476927).

### HHUUD10: Historical Housing Unit and Urbanization Database &middot; [OSF](https://osf.io/fzv5e/) &middot; [Esri Living Atlas](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=98efddc0d7b84ddea6d211f7958447d8)

> Historical housing unit counts and urbanization estimates in consistent census tract boundaries for the continental United States for each decade from 1940 to 2010, plus 2019. Available as csv, dta, xpt, v8xpt, shapefile, and GeoJSON, and as a feature service on Esri&rsquo;s Living Atlas. Methods: [*Scientific Data*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01184-x). [More about HHUUD10](/Projects/HHUUD10/).

### THOLCADS: Tabulated HOLC Area Description Sheets &middot; [OSF](https://osf.io/qytj8/) &middot; [Leventhal Map &amp; Education Center](https://data.leventhalmap.org/#/catalog/dkyajewyh)

> Tabulated data from the Area Description sheets that accompanied the Home Owners&rsquo; Loan Corporation&rsquo;s Residential Security maps. Available as xlsx, csv, shapefile, and GeoJSON, it lets users run qualitative and quantitative assessments of the criteria HOLC used to grade neighborhoods. Methods: [*Environment and Planning B*](https://doi.org/10.1177/23998083221133112). [More about the HOLC project](/Projects/HOLC/).

### [Housing, Socioeconomic, and Racial Data in Consistent Neighborhood Geometries for 65 Cities Mapped by the HOLC, 1940&ndash;2019](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/JXATU)

> Historical census data harmonized to 2010 tracts and HOLC neighborhood boundaries for each census year from 1940 to 2010, plus the 2015&ndash;2019 ACS. Variables include total population; white, Black, and non-white/non-Black population shares; total housing units; the shares of units that are single-family detached, occupied, and owner-occupied; median home value; median contract rent; and median household income. Methods: [*SocArXiv*](https://doi.org/10.31235/osf.io/5w6jx).

### [Tracts by Census Place Using Centroids](https://github.com/snmarkley1/Tracts-by-Census-Place-Using-Centroids) &middot; [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7649459)

> R scripts that assign every US census tract to the Census-defined [place](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/reference/GARM/Ch9GARM.pdf) (a city or town) containing its centroid. Users can build the file for the whole country or for single states, and choose the tract or place vintage (2010 or later). Ready-made csv and xlsx files for 2010 and 2020 tracts with 2021 places are in the `file_download` folder.
